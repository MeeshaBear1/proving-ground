# VOICE: Proving Ground

This repo speaks the house voice ([`portfolio/voice/HOUSE-VOICE.md`](../portfolio/voice/HOUSE-VOICE.md),
v0.8) and extends it as below. Derived 2026-10-10 from this repo's `README.md` and `index.html`,
and from the sibling repo `provingground` (the scoreboard site), which carries the same name, the
same studio and the same claim. The two repos share this contract; keep them in step.

## The three lines

- Claim. Every claim the studio makes about its own work is published with evidence you can
  re-derive without trusting us, and every prediction is scored in public, misses included.
  Taken from `README.md` § *The one rule* and the scoreboard's protocol section.
- Object. A ledger page (this repo) and a scoreboard page (`provingground`). Doors for headings
  and buttons, body for the ledger prose. Ledger rows are data and are never rewritten for voice.
- Reader. Someone checking whether a claim from one of the studio's products holds up: a
  buyer, a reviewer, a journalist, a sceptic. They arrive believing a studio's claims are
  marketing, and they leave with a file path they can open.

## Modes

- Invitation on the hero, the lede and the close. The reader is the subject: *you can check
  this*. The pivot is `so`: a rule we keep, so the reader can re-derive the row.
- Credential on the rule box, the launch section and the row discipline. The work is the
  subject: named files, named tests, dates. The pivot is `by`: a claim earns its row by the
  artifact that backs it.

## Stances

Carries every house stance, the dare close included: the page asks the reader to hold us to
the record, which is a dare by construction. Mutes none.

- Opinion. A claim in launch copy with no ledger row behind it is a bug: the copy says more
  than we can show (`README.md`).
- Refusal. A row is never deleted to look better, and no score is invented to fill an empty
  board. A falsified claim is marked `REFUTED` and stays.
- Admission. The Wave-C audit stopped at 57 of 245 rules when a session hit its usage cap
  (PG-P3), and the scoreboard has scored nothing yet. Both are on the page, in body.

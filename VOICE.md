Derived 2026-10-10 for the fleet voice sweep, from HOUSE-VOICE.md v0.10, this repo's README and the ledger page itself. The house floor applies whole; this file adds the venture.

## Claim
Every claim we make about our own products sits in one public ledger with the evidence a stranger can re-derive, and the ones we cannot yet prove are listed as pending in the same table.

## Reader
A buyer, a journalist, a regulator or an insurer checking whether a launch's superlative has anything behind it; and our own launch writers, who may not ship a claim without a row.

## Register
Credential mode throughout. The table is data: every row's claim, artifact, counts, p-values and dates are quoted verbatim from the cited evidence and are never rewritten for tone. Prose around the table says what the ledger is, how a row is born, and what pending means. No invitation mode; nothing is for sale on this page.

## What Proving Ground never says
- A verdict the artifact does not carry; DRAFT and PENDING stay visible.
- Internal program names, build numbers or planning jargon a reader outside the studio cannot decode.
- "Proven", "certified" or "validated" without the row.
- A row deleted to look better; a falsified claim is marked REFUTED and kept.

## Lexicon it owns
re-derive · executed evidence · UNVERIFIABLE · PENDING · DRAFT · the row · naming the gap.

## Mutes
The dare close is muted, because a ledger closes on how to re-derive a row and has nobody to invite.
